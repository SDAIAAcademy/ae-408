Show results from at least two measured runs. A claim without a number is a plan, not a result.

What full marks look like. Image pipeline: field accuracy and grounding on the test set, with the invented-object probe rate. Speech evaluation: WER by dialect and channel with intervals and the rules named. Voice agent: the per-stage trace of twenty scripted calls against the 1,200 ms budget, and no booking without a confirmed yes. Test set and simulation: the coverage table with its gaps, and calls graded from the store. Decision record: one page, re-run to the same fingerprint. Passing needs 70% overall and at least 60% on this rubric.

Format

10 minutes to present, then 5 minutes of questions

1 · The system · 2 min

Lamha and Rahhal, or your equivalent: the design and the decision record in one slide.

2 · The results · 5 min

Field accuracy, WER by slice, the latency trace, simulated calls: each with its interval and its test set.

3 · One failure · 3 min

One failure from your log: the cause, the mitigation and the number before and after.

In the questions, the assessor gives you a call and a photo you have not seen; run your system on them.

Prepare the assessor's inputs before the day: one short audio file of a booking call with a correction in it, and one trail photo with a face and a sign. Synthetic or consented material only. Peer feedback after each talk: one strength, one question, one suggestion, written.
